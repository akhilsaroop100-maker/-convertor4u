01

Purpose

Convertor4U provides a fast, accessible way to convert measurements between systems while explaining the relationships behind the result. It combines a restrained interface with exact arithmetic, useful formulas, reference tables, and shareable conversion pages.

02

How calculations work

Fixed measurements use deterministic decimal calculations through a category base unit. Formula-based measurements, including temperature, apply explicit scales and offsets. Currency is kept separate and uses dated institutional reference-rate data.

03

Designed for privacy

No account is required. Favorites, recent conversions, and theme preferences are stored locally in your browser and remain on that device unless you clear its site data.
