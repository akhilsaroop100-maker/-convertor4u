01

Device-local data

Favorites, recent conversions, and theme preferences are saved in your browser’s local storage. Convertor4U does not require an account for these features, and this information is not synchronized between devices.

02

Server requests

Like most websites, hosting infrastructure may process basic request information needed to deliver pages securely and diagnose faults. Currency selections and values may be processed by our server to obtain and apply a current reference rate.

03

Your choices

You can remove locally stored history and favorites by clearing site data in your browser. This policy will be updated before adding analytics, advertising, accounts, or other data-processing features.

04

Correction reports

If you submit the corrections and contact form, we store the name you choose to provide, your email address, the reported page, subject, and message so the editorial team can investigate and respond. Do not include sensitive personal information. Reports can be marked resolved by an administrator.
