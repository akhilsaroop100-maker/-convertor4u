Device-local data and server requests

Favorites, recent conversions, and theme preferences are saved in your browser’s local storage and are not synchronized between devices. 03

Google advertising and cookies

When advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers, and similar technologies to serve, measure, limit, and report ads. Google and its advertising partners may use advertising cookies to show ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to Convertor4U or other websites. This can include personalized advertising where the visitor’s consent and applicable law allow it, as well as non-personalized or limited ads.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google’s partner-sites policy. Other authorized advertising vendors or networks may use their own technologies and privacy policies; when they are used, their vendor details and choices are presented through our consent message.

04

Consent choices

For visitors in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Convertor4U uses a Google-certified consent management platform when Google advertising is active. The consent message explains the purposes and vendors involved and lets eligible visitors accept, reject, or manage choices before personalized advertising is used. A privacy-choices link supplied by the consent system allows those choices to be reviewed later.

You can opt out of personalized Google advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some participating third-party vendors through YourAdChoices. Browser settings can block or delete cookies and local storage, although doing so may reset preferences or affect some features.

05

Correction reports

If you submit the corrections and contact form, we store the name you choose to provide, your email address, the reported page, subject, and message so the editorial team can investigate and respond. Do not include sensitive personal information. Reports can be marked resolved by an administrator.

06

Retention, disclosure, and updates

We retain contact reports and essential service logs only as long as reasonably needed for support, security, legal, and operational purposes. Information may be processed by hosting, database, currency-data, security, consent, and advertising providers acting for their stated purposes. We do not sell the contact information submitted through the corrections form. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect the site and its users.
